Keys: left and right arrows step one day, shift and an arrow seven days, space plays or pauses, home and end jump to the ends of the record. Click or drag on the time panels to scrub. Calls: A alive, D or S dead or shed (one class: detection data cannot separate them), U uncertain; each saves and, with advance on call, opens the next animal. N next animal, P previous animal, C the sidebar. D stamps the candidate date when no death date is set; it never stamps the day in view. With no candidate, D saves the call without a date, keeps the card open and asks for one: move the cursor to the death day and press X, or type the date. X sets the death date to the cursor date once the cursor has moved, and moves a date already set; the date field takes a typed date. The keys work after a click on the slider or a checkbox; they pause while you type in a text or date field.
